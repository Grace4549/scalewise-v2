Business model:

ScaleWise has two user types: Clients (business owners seeking help) and Experts (professionals offering paid sessions). Experts apply through an application form (name, email, industry, years of experience) which goes into an admin approval queue. Experts only appear publicly once approved by an admin.
Industries (alphabetical, exactly these 13):

Agriculture & Agribusiness, Beauty & Salons, Construction & Contracting, Education & Training, E-commerce & Retail, Financial Services, Healthcare & Clinics, Hospitality & Tourism, Logistics & Transport, Manufacturing & SMEs, Real Estate, Restaurants & Food Business, Tech Startups
Session types, exactly these three, each with its own commission rate:

Business Discovery — an open conversation for when the client isn't sure of the exact problem, just wants general advice or to talk it through. 20% platform commission.
Consultancy — focused advice for a specific, already-identified problem. 20% commission.
Growth Strategy — a structured long-term engagement offered as either a 3-month or 6-month plan with biweekly sessions. 15% commission.

Client booking flow:

Sign up or log in, search or browse experts by industry (with autocomplete suggestions as they type), select an expert, view their profile (bio, years of experience, rating, reviews, pricing for the three session types), choose a session type, pick a date and time, pay securely, and receive a confirmation email with an automatically generated Google Meet link. After booking, clients and experts can message each other directly inside the platform.
Payments: Support M-Pesa, Airtel Money, and card payments, since this platform serves business owners including those in East Africa.
Reviews: Any visitor, even without an account, can submit a public review (name, business name optional, 1-5 star rating, written review). Reviews are visible to all visitors immediately, no approval needed.
Admin dashboard: Approve or reject expert applications, view all bookings across the platform, see commission earned per booking, and moderate or remove reviews if needed.
