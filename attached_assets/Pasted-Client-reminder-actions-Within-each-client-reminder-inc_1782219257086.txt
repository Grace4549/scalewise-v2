 Expert reminder actions: Within each expert reminder, include two options: "Cancel" and "Request to Reschedule."

If the expert chooses to cancel, notify the client in two ways: an email notification (logged for now, not yet sent) and a visible notification in the Client Dashboard, clearly stating that the expert has cancelled and that the client will receive a full refund, consistent with our policy that an expert cancellation always results in a full refund to the client.

Instead, send a notification to the client (email logged for now, not yet sent, plus a visible notification in the Client Dashboard) informing them that the expert has requested to reschedule, and directing them to their Client Dashboard to select a new available time slot from the expert's calendar.
